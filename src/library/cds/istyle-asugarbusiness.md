The second album to feature Aoki Lapis, released by her parent company i-style Project. It's hard to find information on them as they are mostly inactive - save for [a recent TTS software for her released under CoeAvatar](https://coeavatar.com/update/detail.php?id=1100345) - but i-style was fairly supportive of Lapis in her heyday... she just didn't catch on like they hoped she would. Regardless, she's my second favorite VOCALOID.

Lapis and EDM are a pretty good combo. A sweet, light vocal against powerful and tense tracks... it's unique.
