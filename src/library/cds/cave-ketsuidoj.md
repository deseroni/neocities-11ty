Minor remasters of two sick soundtracks. Ketsui and DOJ, I feel, are some of the most exciting and emotional soundtracks in CAVE's library. Makes sense as they're kind of sibling releases and tackle similar themes of sacrifice and trust. DOJ is cooler than DFK and SDOJ, retro sci-fi theming and all.
