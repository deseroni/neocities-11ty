A narrative album themed around phantom thievery and slapstick fun. YMCK are the Phantom Thieves on pursuit of their "rivals," The Swingers. They even have their own theme songs! A lighthearted but rich listen from beginning to end.

I first found YMCK through a Yugioh ZEXAL MAD many years ago. I'm surprised their following continues to be small, but they're still trucking. Send them overseas love if you're able.
