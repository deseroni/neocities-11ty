*Could you show me dear, something I've not seen, something infinitely interesting*

Compared to their earlier albums, this fare is mellow, reflective, quite removed from their 90's grunge rock. You can tell the members have matured, become wiser... Honestly, I'm having trouble describing this album in detail. It's just amazing.

My dad owns this release on vinyl and happened to play it when I first wrote this page.
