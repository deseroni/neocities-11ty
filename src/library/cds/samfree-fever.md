LOVE YOU, LOVE YOU, ONLY YOU

This is Pure Eurobeat Heaven, or Fever, a survey of samfree's VOCALOID works. Half of the tracklist consists of his Night series, with one new song and high-energy Eurobeat remixes of old tracks like Daybreak, Promise, and Dark-Colored Alice. All of the songs are structurally very similar, an unfortunate weakness, but samfree had a strong grip on the genre. He also played a role in popularizing upcoming V2/V3 voicebanks, and their Fever entires are still beloved today.
