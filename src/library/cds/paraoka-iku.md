The first album to employ erotic vocal Acme Iku as a singer, by the only musician who has ever done so. Thanks to this, she is hardly comprehensible, but it is still a novel and impressive product. It's made to shock the listener through its lyrics, Iku's voice, and the awareness that a product such as Iku even exists. I think she's adorable, but many of us were exposed to her too young.

I recommend this if you're itching to listen to something fresh and surprising. Two bonus tracks are absent from the Bandcamp release, petal-go-round(demo) being a replacement bonus. It was later completed and featured in paraoka's future album, Zenmashi.
