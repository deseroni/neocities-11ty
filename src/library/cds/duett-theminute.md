When two 80s pop specialists collab, it's gonna be dangerously addicting. This album's moody, sometimes melancholy and wistful but overall determined, with the melancholic tracks recalling Phil Collins. Yatte's light and bright vocals fit the theming, and I loved the thick chorus effect in "She Told Me So." Catchy melodies, succint lyrics, and heavy reverb. Super good! (Albeit an acquired taste...)
