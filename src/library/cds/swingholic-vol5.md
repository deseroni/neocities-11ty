One of many Touhou arrangement albums by SOUND HOLIC's jazzy circle SWING HOLIC. Despite the rapid pace at which they were released, SWING HOLIC albums were generally worth their while. You can't go wrong with a live jazz ensemble or SOUND HOLIC, in itself a talent magnet. Not to mention the packaging and associated music videos are killer. My favorite is track 7, L→R KOMACHI.
