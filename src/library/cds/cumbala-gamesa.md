A free CD included in a box of Gamesa cookies! I think my grandma offloaded it on me randomly but I never ate them, so the box sat neglected in a pantry for many years. One day I was cleaning out that pantry and was curious about the state of the cookies, only to notice this CD on top of the tray.

The first track is a loving introduction from Gamesa, priding itself in its history, your brand loyalty, and wishing you a Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year. The rest are 6 silly covers of traditional Christmas songs like Vamos, Pastores and A Belen Pastores. Noche de Paz is an original mix of the song of the same name, Ya viene la media noche uses the Deck the Halls melody, and Suenan y las campanas is set to Jingle Bells. Nothing mindblowing but unlike popular versions of these songs.

All audio is in Spanish.
