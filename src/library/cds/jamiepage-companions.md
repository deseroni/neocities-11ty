A fresh album on self-image, freedom, and of course, love. The affectionate leitmotif that echoes throughout the album unites it to great effect. Jamie's lyrics might not be to everyone's taste... I'd call hers "raw:" intense emotions pulled back a bit by silly, even corny expressions. But I also respect the boldness.
Long but fun listen.
