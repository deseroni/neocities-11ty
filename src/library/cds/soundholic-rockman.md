Happy 36th anniversary to the Mega Man series and 30th for Mega Man X. Here's one of the best remix albums around and with an insane lineup to boot. The jacket and promotional materials are misleading, though... all but two arrangements are of Classic Mega Man tracks. Hopefully this doesn't deter from an otherwise cool love letter. The 16th track, RELOADED, is an original work that fits the vibe of the album well.
