Paraoka and Haru*nya's fourth album. Paraoka is a brilliant composer and possibly insane. Don't be fooled by the electronic instruments, it's closer to improv/jazz. Longtime collaborator Haru*nya gives the album a surreal sound with almost impossibly clear vocals. The slight nasalness and grounds the craziness. "monolithize phases" is one of the best opening tracks, ever.
