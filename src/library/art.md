## Art Resources

### General

+ [Adobe Stock](https://stock.adobe.com/photos)
+ [DEATH TO STOCK](https://www.deathtothestockphoto.com/)
    + Subscription required
+ [College Art Association Reviews](http://www.caareviews.org/)
+ [Art21](https://art21.org)
    + Short films and articles about contemporary artists
+ [Juxtapoz Magazine](https://juxtapoz.com)
    + San Franciscco-based contemporary art publication
+ [WhiteChapel Gallery's Documents of Contemporary Art](https://shop.whitechapelgallery.org/collections/documents-of-contemporary-art?sort_by=manual)
    + Collections of 20th to 21st century essays relevant to selected topics
+ [The Brooklyn Rail](https://thebrooklynrail.org)
    + Critical essays and reviews of art/ists
+ [Art-collecting.com](https://art-collecting.com)
    + Index and information for US gallery activities, advice for collectors and artists
+ [Underpaint Academy](https://underpaintacademy.com)
    + Paid courses and lecture contents
+ [Jarret Fuller: readings.design](https://readings.design)
    + Large repository of graphic design books

### The Figure

+ [Adorkastock](https://adorkastock.com/pose)
+ [Quickposes](https://quickposes.com/en)
    + Timed figure drawing and reference photos
+ [Comparing Heights](https://www.mrinitialman.com/OddsEnds/Sizes/compsizes.xhtml)
    + Custom rough estimates of height differences between figures in cm and in.
+ [hikaku sitatter](https://hikaku-sitatter.com/en/)
    + Similar to Comparing Heights
+ [Michael Lauricella's "Morpho" books](https://www.thriftbooks.com/a/michel-lauricella/3585572/)
    + Intermediate level reference books for anything relating to the figure. They're tiny but full of wisdom and examples
+ [George Bridgman: Bridgman's Complete Guide to Drawing from Life](https://archive.org/download/pdfy-72f-FzW7wYN_r0ny/Bridgman%20-%20Complete%20Guide%20to%20Drawing%20from%20Life_text.pdf)
