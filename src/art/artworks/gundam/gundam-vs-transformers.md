these are gijinka for a transformers/gundam crossover [@ectobeing](https://deviantart.com/ectobeing) and i played with 😂 she designed all of the decepticons, while i made the mobile suits, which are not based off of any one pilot. however they are lightly inspired by the GunPla-kun shorts.

main idea: the mobile suits, a group of deserters during the one year war (or an equivalent where humans aren't involved in combat), become involved with the decepticons and later the autobots, who are dealing with their own energy crisis back on cybertron and turn to Earth for resources. they join the decepticons for a time, but nothing ever goes well with them.
