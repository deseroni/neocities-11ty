an unexpected update has been made to the Guest Star ??? mode in Kirby Star Allies, adding only one character: a cybernetic diva who hopes to move galaxies with her incredible singing voice!

fanart of my friend Kc's design of Miku stylized as a Kirby character! post-RTDL partner designs are more humanoid than before--the most human up to that point was an actual human, Ado--so it seemed like an easy enough transition. i took it to an extreme and added a Factory Tyrant-based design if she were to become corrupted or attack Kirby and co. at first i thought the handcuff hair accessories would clutter too much, but their omission would leave her chest CD to be the only silver accessory.

Kc's original work is [https://x.com/kcdoos/status/1744170940609581464/photo/1](here)

7 hours (4 for planning, 3ish for painting). i'm not used to these kinds of doodle pages, so it took longer to compose.
