heartwarming: i took two early 20th-century orphan girls on a vacation in canada

this is a simple reference piece. i've always wanted to do the "draw over photos" thing, so i'm using photos i took in canada last summer, with the girls in these outfits. tip and i photographed our (real) emilico and kate plushes throughout so it's like bringing that to life.

kate's wearing a dress from a 1910's catalogue and emilico's is dated at 1890's, though it has the shape of something a bit newer. around that period, american childrens' clothing deviated from adults' clothing, and freedom of movement was factored into designs. low waistlines, straight sleeves, and sailor-styled tops...
