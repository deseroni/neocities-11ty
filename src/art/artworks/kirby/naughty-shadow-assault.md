the only thing worse than one Shadow Kirby is 13 Shadow Kirbys.

this is my piece for the Kirby Artbook project [https://x.com/KirbyArtbook](https://x.com/KirbyArtbook) that i did a bit of modding for. the submission period was very generous; i began this in June. usually, for fan projects like this, the deadline is extended much further than planned lol. unfortunately i do not know when a printed or digital version of the book will be available, but i will update this as soon as i hear anything.

10/9/22: you can view some submissions here: [https://x.com/search?q=KirbyArtbook](https://x.com/search?q=KirbyArtbook)

most of the Shadow Kirbys call references to certain appearances. for example, the four hatless Copy Abilities seen are what he uses when you encounter him in Amazing Mirror, and two represent real-life plushes from the Shadow Kirby lines :)
