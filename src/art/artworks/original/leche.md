a little thing of my character, Leche. she's a sentient painting that was dearly beloved by her late painter... i'll leave it at that!! she's not evil but says bizarre and concerning things sometimes. she's a mix of elements: Western doll-like face and hair, an attire that's half-everyday Mexican skirt + blouse combo, half-pure white color and frills you see on Bomba/Plena dancers, and weird personality. some of those skirts get really ornate with lots of ribbons underneath, but this is simple.

took at least 5 hrs because the initial work was not up to par and i spent a lot of time painting over it
