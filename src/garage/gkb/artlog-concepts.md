## Concept Art

A look into my teenage psyche. I'm sorry.

### 2013

![](../../images/gkb/ref%20sheet%20for%20cos.png)

Before Kirby was actually Kirby and Prince Fluff cosplay ideas for my friend PSP and I. To give credit to my young self, she understood that closet cosplays were much more viable for us. I still have red pants and an unstyled pink wig that were intended for it.

![](../../images/gkb/output_qZIipX.gif)

Random Magolor gijinka. Looking a bit pale. Used him as reference later on.

![](../../images/gkb/KIRBU.png)

The earlier interest in Kirby gijinka manifested within my friends at the time, and this was my friend Genny's design of him. He appeared in our roleplays between Meta Knight, Len Kagamine, and GUMI. (yep.)

My interests were all trumped by Mega Man after 2013, but I drew a ton of gi for other series in between.
