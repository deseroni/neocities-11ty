A royal guard-in-training who grew up in Castle Dedede due to his parents' jobs as guards there. Functionally King Dedede's butler but he doesn't seem to mind due to his admiration for the king. His openmindedness makes him popular but often gets him tied up in Kirby and co.'s antics, which exhaust and frustrate him. He really does try to empathize with everyone, but it's a tall task in Dreamland. Overall a good kid that wants to impress his idols.

He's good friends with Gooey and encourages him to step out of his comfort zone and befriend others. They sign up as a team during a tournament in Dreamland (Kirby Fighters 2).

### Notes

+ His mom and dad met at work.
+ The royal guard had little to do in the past, so his dedication to an "easy" job was quickly noticed.
+ He reacts surprisingly poorly to Dark Matter possession, becoming frantic and unfocused.
+ He is the least prepared of the group for cosmic horrors beyond comprehension.
