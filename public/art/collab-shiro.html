Tags: #Bomberman

Artist Comments:

Bomber hurl!

my second work for the Bomberman 40th collaboration. with the semester over, i've had all weekend to draw fun stuff. (11 hours in 2 days kind of time.) i'll get back to canvas/panel paintings soon, but for now i'm in a groove.

i've been very excited about this opportunity because it feels like a long time coming... i've sketched bombergirl stuff on and off for two years but none of it amounted to color illustrations, which are what i prefer to post. (the series could always use more SFW art.) so i went all out.
