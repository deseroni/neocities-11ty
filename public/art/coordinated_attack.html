big order for my friends Muggz, Chom, and their cats. i started it shortly before going on vacation, so wrapping it up took longer than i expected haha. but it's always nice to draw Splatoon stuff, specially for someone i play with often. because i don't have experience drawing animals, the cats took longer to nail; i studied cats for a bit, then adapted them to be at somewhat Splatoon-y. Judd and Lil Judd are simple, fuzzy, and round, so i figured slightly realistic forms were a good inbetween.
