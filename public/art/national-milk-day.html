Artist Comments:

1/11 is National Milk Day in the USA. It celebrates the safe bottling and delivery of milk to the general population ^_^

i saw a local grocer promoting a deal on milk for the day, so i thought, this is a good time to draw Leche with her namesake! as well as use some brushes by Pipri https://profile.clip-studio.com/en-us/profile/roefj9ze-kg. i live in a major agricultural region so i'm accustomed to ranches and farms. and cows are my favorite farm animals, very sweet and expressive
