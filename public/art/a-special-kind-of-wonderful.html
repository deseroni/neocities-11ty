fanart i've had on the backburner since feburary. originally intended as a valentine's piece, it was pushed back while working on my yunosuke arts and recovering from painting for 3 months straight. i returned to it again after completing the commissions i've shared here. (i'm a "work on one project at a time" type of worker, i really don't like juggling things if i can help it.)

based on episode 36 of wonderful precure and the beautiful sunset under which iroha confesses her feelings for satoru. strong end to a good episode and arc <3 i was reviewing some of Omiya Io's illustrations at the time and was compelled to try a zoomed out/angular composition like his.

time: ~9hrs over past two weeks + 2-3 hours sometime between the sketch and color blocking
