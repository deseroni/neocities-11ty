the Ultimate Lifeform-sama

i guess this is my celebratory post-spring-semester work? took ~10+ hours over a few days and was fun to work on. action poses are not my strong suit yet, so i thought i should try something unfamiliar. pose is based on the third swing of elfilis' basic combo, the one that might catch you off guard if you don't know its rhythm

this design was mostly final up to now but i accessorized her left leg mid-drawing for extra flair. i leaned more into lis' overall grace and elegance than the furry stuff, though she has a tail like her other half. she's leagues above everyone else; look, don't touch, and accept your beautiful death
