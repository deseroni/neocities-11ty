Protect the Queen!

Published: December 25, 2023
Tags: #Kirby
Full View
Artist Comments:

with your trusty pepperbox!

my Secret Santa gift for SomethingInWorl www.tumblr.com/somethinginworl, for the Kirby Gijinka Center Discord event. they have interesting and nuanced interpretations of Ribbon and the Queen, which you don't see often in gijinka circles, so i wanted to show my appreciation somehow. Ribbon is a gun nut... come on, that's cool. i don't have much experience with intense/'off-screen horror' scenes like this, so i wanted to see if i could bend light and color to sell such a feeling.

~8.5 hours, fast all things considered
