Pro Memoria 2024

Published: November 2, 2024
Tags: #Original
Full View
Artist Comments:

16" x 20", acrylic on canvas

i think this is my first physical painting upload to this account? it really was digital painting only until now. for anyone who doesn't know, though, i was trained in painting.

a return to my Pro Memoria series and the first work in the series painted physically! a year-old concept i jumped into with new fresh feelings, while still centered around Dia de Los Muertos

i visit roadside memorials pretty often and even help maintain one so sights of candles forming a powerful glow resonate with me. this painting and the candle line extends to the edges; i imagined it stretching on beyond what's visible. candles are such a bold way of showing you're thinking or someone or something

happy Dia de Los Muertos!!!
