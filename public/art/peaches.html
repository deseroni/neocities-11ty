Peaches the Piplup

Published: October 19, 2023
Tags: #Pokemon
Full View
Artist Comments:

Reaching for the top!

a rare non-legendary gijinka of my childhood partner Peaches, who i repurposed slightly to be Dawn's partner. i don't usually design these because they operate on a different logic in my mind: only legendaries or mythicals can utilize a human form, so gijinkas of others would have to exist in another world. this is how i imagine her there. an adventurous but flashy type that's always ready to show off.
