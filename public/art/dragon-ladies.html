Dragon Ladies

Tags: #Pokemon

Artist Comments:

happy year of the Dragon, what better time to draw some Dragon Ladies? the best excuse so far to draw gijinka waifus. the smug colonial, the perky Satan, and the exhausted hag. Reshiram's the only one of that causes real trouble.

i had a random voice telling me i should use the Splatfest pen from the Pufuu Splatoon set i've frequented; i love the results and will definitely be using it more for loose digital paintings. this was difficult to time since i had clip open while working on an acrylic painting throughout the week, but let's say ~6-7 hours.
