Leche and the territorial Canada Geese.

the coast looked clear until she walked thru this family of geese which the mother took offense to (honestly, the mother is a bit small, Canada Geese can be 100+ cm at times). my campus is home to many of these geese and signs are left out to avoid them during mating season (right now). that structure in the background is the backside of the outdoors theater stage.

this one took abnormally long to complete because of how demanding it was of my focus; so many small elements to round out. not a hard process just a very very long one, around 14 hours long. i'm a complete workaholic these days. ENJOY
