Emerald/FRLG-Pt era art, anyway

4hr exercise and private art stream practice. Takie (May's Blaziken) and Gwan (Brendan's Gardevoir) are just Pokemon usually, but i really wanted to draw their gijinka forms as psuedo-official character art. they look like passable NPCs, i think. think of these forms as how the two imagine themselves. next time i try this, i'm thinking of either FRLG Mew or RGBY Selene
