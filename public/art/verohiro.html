Veronika and Hiro

Published: January 18, 2023
Tags: #Splatoon
Full View
Artist Comments:

i don't know what they're looking at

this was a random painting i started before the new year and only returned to it last night for ~4 hours :D i love the mascot characters for 3, maybe even more than the OGs Kaori and John. their amiibo dialogues are funny. i imagine Veronika is a playground bully type and Hiro never gives her the reactions she craves. he's a mommy's boy

i like the result but i can't help but feel like i tighten up my paintings to an extreme. i can't capture the looseness of official Spl art this way. need to make those technical improvements this year
