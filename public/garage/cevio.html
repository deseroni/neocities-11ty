Tohoku Kiritan Projects

In Summer of 2022, I bit on a DLSite sale and purchased Tohoku Kiritan for $90... in the dead of night, I'll get excited and start making covers. Everything heard here is mixed by a friend. Kiritan is usually set to +0.20 maturity.

The AI used in CEVIO/Voisona is proprietary. It utilizes neural network learning on samples of the voice providers to alter results when the synthesized voice is rendered, like pitch, speed, and breathiness. Read here.

Old Covers (ver. 1.1.0)

Deep Sea Girl (深海少女) - yuuyu-P

My first CEVIO AI cover with base Kiritan. It took months to realize I had not updated to her 1.1.0 version. As far as a first cover goes I am pleased with it, but it's overworked. This was after a number of attempts to correct the excessive edits.

Fukashigi no Carte (不可思議のカルテ) - Kawai Luigi, Kodama Ameko

It took much more effort for Kiritan to sing this passably as she needed multiple timing, pitch, and vibrato adjustments. I took advantage of the then-new huskiness slider to convey the softness of the original vocalists.

Kiritan's vowel -> i notes are on an early VOCALOID2 level of choppy without adjustment. And she just loves to drift off key.

Sunao ni Narenai (素直になれない) - momo

Unfinished demo, but I think it suits Kiritan's character.
