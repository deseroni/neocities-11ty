Elfilin

  • Age: equiv. to 14
  • Height: 135cm / 4'5
  • Species: ???
  • Favorite food: Melon

An intelligent girl found alone in the New World. Wants to be helpful but is terribly self-conscious and hides her powers. She lived alone long before Kirby and co. arrived, foraging for resources as needed. Despite her supportive role, she acts suspiciously so no one discovers her connection to the incidents in the New World.

Technically the positive "half" of Elfilis. She formed over time as Elfilis absorbed "positive" dreams from the stars she destroyed, and their philosophies naturally clashed. Being captured and experimented on by the inhabitants of the New World accelerated her desperation to split, but she had to endure experiments in her newly split form until she could figure out how to escape. It left her with a fear of medical environments.

Notes

  • Her jacket and shoes are from Alivel Mall and are still in good shape.
  • She and Elfilis have pointed ears. Her right ear is clipped, and she covers it out of embarrassment with her bangs.
  • Meta gets terrible deja vu from her due to her background being similar to Kirby's.
