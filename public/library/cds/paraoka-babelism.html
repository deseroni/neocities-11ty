Koujimachi-Ongen - Babelism

  • Release date: 8/13/2010
  • Genre: Electronic, rock, chiptune, jazz
  • Featuring: Paraoka (music), Haru*nya (vocals)
  • Runtime: 5 tracks, 25:30

Paraoka and Harunya's fourth album. Paraoka is a brilliant composer and possibly insane. Don't be fooled by the electronic instruments, it's closer to improv/jazz. Longtime collaborator Harunya gives the album a surreal sound with almost impossibly clear vocals. The slight nasalness and grounds the craziness. "monolithize phases" is one of the best opening tracks, ever.
