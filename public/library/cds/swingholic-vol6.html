SWING HOLIC VOL.6

  • Release date: 12/30/2010
  • Genre: Swing, jazz
  • Featuring: Swing Holic Band, DAUGHTER, Sanokan, A~YA (vocals and lyrics), Nana Takahashi, SYO
  • Runtime: 11 tracks, 45:56

One of many Touhou arrangement albums by SOUND HOLIC's jazzy circle SWING HOLIC. This time, it's arrangements of songs from Subterranean Animism, a new mainline game at the time. And among my favorite games in the series albeit difficult. Very similar to VOL.05 in execution, so it's much of the same... but is that always a bad thing? Standout tracks include Take Fire, A Song of Eyelids, Light and Love, and decolorize.
