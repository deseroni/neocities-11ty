Namitape - Flitter

  • Release date: 3/25/2023
  • Genre: Synthwave, dreampop
  • Featuring: Kaai Yuki, SynthV Mai (vocals)
  • Runtime: 17 tracks, 56:51

An ethereal and nostalgic experience, vocal tracks with sweet ambient tunes in between. the track order feels as it should, but if I had to be picky, Yaruki ga nai is more MAD-y than the rest.

namitape's my favorite Yuki user next to inabakumori, of which they are likely a disciple. They're multi-disciplinary, collaborative, and have a distinctive but evolving identity.
