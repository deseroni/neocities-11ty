TsugiEdgi - Momentwake

  • Release date: 12/15/2025
  • Genre: DnB, breakcore
  • Featuring: TsugiEdgi
  • Runtime: 7 tracks (10 bonus), 28:20 (55:35)

Mesmerizing album by a SilvaGunner collaborator? Such a background explains their insane range of genres, and the nagging feeling that this could all work as video game music... anyway, this is meticulous, perfectly ordered, and successful in its goal of moving the listener to action. It sways to and fro, soft and ambient to frantic drums, like following someone through the motions of the day, or building up the confidence to go for something. I really love it.

If they ever see this: I still think about your old unfinished Super Monkey Ball remix.
