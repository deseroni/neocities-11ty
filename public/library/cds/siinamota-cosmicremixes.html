siinamota - because, cosmic remixes.

  • Release date: 6/30/2023
  • Genre: Electronica, melodic metal, chiptune
  • Featuring: Hatsune Miku (vocal), siinamota (Vocal), PinocchioP, KTG, UtsuP, sasakure.UK
  • Runtime: 4 tracks, 16:41

Remix album based on unfinished works of the late siinamota (1995-2015), who was signed to GINGA. A stretch goal for a crowdfund to cover production costs of an album release party for the 2019 siinamota album, "Therefore." Both albums are difficult but inspiring listens. I want to create as rigorously as he did, and as his colleagues do.
