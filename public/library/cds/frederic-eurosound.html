Frédéric Messent - european sound community

  • Release date: 2001
  • Genre: House, trance
  • Featuring: Taxi, Pascal, Sarah Jane Morris, dZIhan & KamIen, Mikaël Delta, Marschmellows, Nu-Spirit Helinski, Black Maybe, Negrocan, Markus Enochson, Frank Adhal (vocals)
  • Runtime: 10 tracks, 1:14:37

Mixed and compiled by Messent, a compiler/manager based in France. A sample of musicians based in England, France, Belgium, Finland, and more, mostly active in the 90s. It's wonderfully packaged, with a bold limited palette and photographs from an airport to express traveling to a new location and engaging with its culture. Each track segues into the next well (with style differences in mind the transitions vary in length), making for a relaxing evening jam. Closes out very funky.
