Jamie Page - Constant Companions (Deluxe Edition)

  • Release date: 7/11/2025
  • Genre: Synthpop, breakbeat
  • Featuring: telebasher, OK Glass, Marcy Nabors/Shadolith, Marlow Jacobs (collaborators), Kasane Teto, GUMI, ANRI, Solaria, Adachi Rei (vocals)
  • Runtime: 17 tracks, 1:09:37

A fresh album on self-image, freedom, and of course, love. The affectionate leitmotif that echoes throughout the album unites it to great effect. Jamie's lyrics might not be to everyone's taste... I'd call hers "raw:" intense emotions pulled back a bit by silly, even corny expressions. But I also respect the boldness. Long but fun listen.
